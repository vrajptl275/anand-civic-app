About Anand Civic Issues

A smart platform for civic issue reporting in Anand city, Gujarat.

Our system enables citizens to report issues like garbage accumulation, road damage, water leakages, and more directly to municipal authorities. With GPS validation and smart department matching, we ensure only legitimate issues within Anand city are processed.

  • Reports validated within Anand city boundary
  • Smart department matching using keywords
  • Real-time status tracking
  • Before/after photo evidence
