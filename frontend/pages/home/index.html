The Process

Simple. Transparent. Fast.

How your report travels from phone to fixed.

  1. 01

    Report

    Snap a photo, drop a pin, describe the issue — done in under a minute.

  2. 02

    Assign

    Smart routing sends your ticket to exactly the right municipal department.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Field officers are dispatched and you see live status updates as they work.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Receive photo proof of the fix. Not satisfied? Reopen with one tap.
